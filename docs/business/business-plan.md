# 🚀 Go-To-Market Framework

## Step 1: Define your launch audience (Beachhead)

Pick the **smallest viable audience** you can dominate first.

- Who are they? (job title, industry, geography, budget)
- Where do they hang out? (platforms, communities, events)
- What language do they use to describe their problem?

## Step 2: Choose 1–2 primary channels

| Channel | Best for | Cost |
|---------|----------|------|
| **Cold outreach** (email/DM) | B2B services, freelancing | Free (time) |
| **Content/SEO** | Long-term inbound, authority building | Free (time) |
| **Social media** | Personal brand, community-driven products | Free (time) |
| **Paid ads** | Validated offer, fast testing | $$ |
| **Partnerships/referrals** | Complementary audiences | Free/rev-share |
| **Marketplaces** (Upwork, Fiverr, Gumroad) | Freelancing, digital products | Platform fee |
| **Community participation** | Niche authority, warm leads | Free (time) |

**Rule:** Master ONE channel before adding another. Spreading thin = failing everywhere.

## Step 3: Launch sequence

1. **Week 1–2:** Soft-launch to 10–20 hand-picked people. Get feedback. Iterate.
2. **Week 3–4:** Refine offer + messaging based on feedback. Collect testimonials.
3. **Week 5+:** Scale the working channel. Add a second channel only after #1 is repeatable.

# 💰 Pricing Basics

## Principles
- Price on **value delivered**, not hours spent (where possible).
- Your first price is almost always too low. Test raising it.
- Anchor high, offer tiers. Let the customer self-select.
- Free = invisible. Charge something from day one (even $1 validates demand).

## Quick formula (services)

```
Floor    = (Monthly living costs + business costs) / billable hours available
Target   = Floor × 1.5–2.0 (accounts for non-billable time, taxes, savings)
Ceiling  = Value delivered to client / 10  (aim for 10× ROI for them)
```

## Three-tier template (products or packages)

| Tier | Name | What's included | Price |
|------|------|-----------------|-------|
| Basic | _Starter_ | Core deliverable only | $__ |
| Standard | _Professional_ | Core + extras (faster, more revisions, support) | $__ |
| Premium | _Enterprise_ | Everything + priority, strategy, custom work | $__ |

Most buyers pick the middle tier. The premium tier makes the middle look reasonable.
